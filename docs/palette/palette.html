Art direction · approved palette

Blocky Assets Palette

One colour system for the whole library. Every surface is earthy and weathered, the way the medieval village already is. Brutes, undead and hell each get a narrow hue band so they read as their own world, and strong saturation is reserved for things that give off light: fire, spells, glowing eyes, enchantments.

Status: version 2 (2026-10-20): less saturation, hue-shifted shadows and highlights, darker creatures. Source of truth: src/core/palette.js; check with node tools/check-palette.mjs.
01 · What is wrong today

Measured from the code

I read every hex colour in src/ (about 2,600) and measured saturation (HSB S) per family. The medieval village is the reference look. Hell, spells and loot sit far above it, and the hell kit uses near-neon colours on 28% of its swatches. The undead surfaces are fine, but their glows use six different neon hues (blue, green, purple, red, gold, white), so the faction has no signature colour.

02 · The rules

Six rules every colour follows

Each swatch on this page belongs to one of three tiers. The page checks every swatch against its tier; a value outside its tier would show in red.

MatterS ≤ 45% · B 7–85% · creatures shaded darker

Anything that does not glow: stone, wood, soil, skin, fur, bone, metal. Weathered and earthy in every faction.

Dye and metalS ≤ 55% · B ≤ 85%

Painted, dyed or polished things: cloth, banners, roof tiles, war paint, gold, bronze. Allowed to be richer than matter, never glowing.

Lightcore S ≤ 75% · highlight S 5–35%

Only emissive things: flames, lava, spells, eyes, runes, enchantments. A light is always a 5-step ramp from a tinted white-hot to a deep ember, so bloom never clips to flat white.

No pure black or whitedarkest #16120e · lightest matter #e8dec0

Replaces #000000, #08060a, #ffffff and friends. Keeps shadows warm and lets bloom do the brightening.

One signature light per factionvillage amber · brute torch · grave green · hell ember

Eye and glow options stay, but they are drawn from the shared ramps so every option sits at the same brightness and saturation.

Shared materials stay sharedone iron, one gold, one bone, one leather

A gold coin, a lich's diadem and the efreet's sash use the same gold ramp. Today there are four golds (#e0b840, #e6b832, #c8a050, #ffb02a).

03 · The four worlds side by side

Same value ladder, different hue

Read across a row: the ground, skin or light of each faction sits at a comparable brightness and saturation, only the hue family changes. This is what makes a goblin, a ghoul and an imp look like they belong to one game.

11 · After approval

How I will roll it out

  1. Put the palette in one file, src/core/palette.js (PAL.fire, PAL.gold, PAL.hellSkin.crimson…), loaded first, and write docs/PALETTE.md with these rules.
  2. Hell kit and hell creatures: skins, brimstone, lava and every fire glow onto the shared ramps.
  3. Spells and effects: all twelve schools onto the ramps above; no pure white highlights.
  4. Undead: grave-light green as the signature, eye and gem options remapped to the shared glow ramps.
  5. Brutes, loot rarity, shared metals; then a check script that flags any new hex outside its tier.
  6. Look at every family in day, dusk and night, run check-character where rigs are touched, then commit and push per phase.

Medieval buildings, villagers and animals stay as they are: they are the reference.
